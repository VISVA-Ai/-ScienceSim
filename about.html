About ScienceSim

ScienceSim is a premium web-based educational platform designed to transform how high school students learn physics and chemistry. Our mission is simple: make complex scientific concepts accessible and engaging through real-time, interactive simulations.

Traditional textbooks show static diagrams. Videos show someone else's experiments. But with ScienceSim, you are the scientist. Adjust parameters, observe outcomes, and develop intuition for how the natural world works.

Our Philosophy

We believe that understanding comes through exploration. That's why every simulation in ScienceSim gives you full control over the variables. Want to see what happens when you increase gravity on a pendulum? Do it. Curious about how temperature affects reaction rates? Test it yourself.

Key Features

  • Real-Time Physics Engine — Frame-rate independent simulations using proper SI units
  • Beautiful Visualizations — Premium dark theme with smooth animations and trajectory predictions
  • Intuitive Controls — Adjust every parameter with responsive sliders and instant feedback
  • Live Data Display — See calculations update in real-time as you experiment
  • Mobile Friendly — Learn anywhere with our responsive design
  • Free Forever — Education should be accessible to everyone

For Students

Whether you're preparing for exams, working on homework, or just curious about how things work, ScienceSim gives you a virtual laboratory at your fingertips. No safety goggles required.

For Teachers

Use ScienceSim to demonstrate concepts in class or assign simulations for homework. Each simulation is designed to illustrate specific curriculum topics for grades 10-12.

Technical Details

ScienceSim is built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript—no frameworks, no dependencies. This means blazing-fast load times and compatibility with any modern browser. The physics engines use proper delta-time calculations for consistent behavior across different devices and frame rates.

Get Started

Ready to explore? Head to our Physics Lab or Chemistry Lab to begin your scientific journey. Happy experimenting!
